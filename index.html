Hi there! I am currently working as a Research Engineer at Haruno Lab at NICT CiNet, advised by Masahiko Haruno. and also conducting research as a Research Assistant at Amano Lab at The University of Tokyo, advised by Ayumu Yamashita.

Previously, I received my MSc in Human Factors Engineering (Distinction) from the Human Engineered Environment Studies at The University of Tokyo, advised by Satori Hachisuka. Prior to that, I majored in signal processing in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Tokyo University of Science.
